# SIMILARITY SCORING

Do not simply average random algorithm percentages.

The primary overall similarity score should represent the approximate percentage of eligible submitted text covered by meaningful matching spans.

General idea:

matched eligible words / total eligible words * 100

Deduplicate overlapping matches before counting.

Then use n-gram, fingerprint, Jaccard and cosine calculations to determine whether a candidate passage qualifies as a meaningful match.

Maintain separate scores for:

* Repository similarity
* Web similarity
* Academic-source similarity
* Overall similarity

The overall score should not double-count the same submitted words simply because multiple sources contain the same passage.

Provide a deterministic, testable implementation.
